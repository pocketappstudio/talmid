Privacy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

What the app stores

Your progress, streaks and settings are saved only on your device. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking cookies. We don't sell your information.

Unlocking

When you enter a license key, the app sends that key to Gumroad to check it, and saves the result on your device. Purchases are handled by Gumroad, which collects your name, email and payment details under its own privacy policy. We receive the order details Gumroad shares with sellers.

Hosting

The app is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log basic technical data like IP addresses for security, under its own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Clearing the app's site data in your browser, or removing the app from your home screen, erases your progress.

Children

Talmid is not directed to children under 13.

Your choices

To ask about your purchase information, message us through Gumroad.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above.
