## Galatians word set — ESV renderings (added 2026-10-22)
Each card now shows the Strong's KJV gloss AND how ESV renders the word in Gal 1:10.
Alignment from Greek interlinear (https://biblehub.com/text/galatians/1-10.htm) to ESV text (https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Galatians+1:10&version=ESV):
- ἀνθρώπους πείθω → "seeking the approval of man" (peitho = "seeking the approval of"; Bible Hub interlinear glosses "do I seek approval")
- ζητῶ ἀνθρώποις ἀρέσκειν → "am I trying to please man" (zeteo = "trying"; aresko = "please")
- ἤρεσκον → "were still trying to please" (aresko)
- Θεόν → "God"; Χριστοῦ δοῦλος → "servant of Christ" (ESV footnote on doulos refers to its Preface)
The word-to-word alignment is my reading of the interlinear against the ESV, not a published ESV interlinear.
